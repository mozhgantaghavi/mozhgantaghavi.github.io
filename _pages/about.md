<p class="pp-lead">I am an applied mathematician who enjoys learning, solving problems and doing research. My main interest is to connect mathematical theory with modern computational methods and artificial intelligence. Over the years, I have become more and more drawn to machine learning and data-driven approaches, because they allow us to turn mathematical ideas into practical answers for real-world problems.</p>

<p>Right now, I work on bringing mathematics and optimization together with AI-based techniques, in order to understand complex systems better. I like to approach a problem from both the analytical side and the computational side, and what motivates me most is the challenge of turning an abstract concept into a method that gives insight, prediction or real impact.</p>
