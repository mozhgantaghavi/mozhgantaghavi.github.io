<p class="pp-lead">Alongside my mathematics studies, I have followed independent courses in <b>programming</b>, <b>machine learning</b> and <b>deep learning</b>. I started by strengthening my programming, mainly in <b>Python</b>, which gave me the base I needed to enter <b>artificial intelligence</b>. After that I widened my learning to many machine learning and deep learning topics: supervised and unsupervised methods, regression and classification, clustering, dimensionality reduction and neural network architectures. I practised all of them hands-on with <b>scikit-learn</b> and <b>PyTorch</b>. This step-by-step path gave me a solid theoretical understanding, strong coding skills and the ability to apply computational and data-driven methods to complex problems.</p>
