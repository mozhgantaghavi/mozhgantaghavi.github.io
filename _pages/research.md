<div class="pp-section"><i class="fa-solid fa-lightbulb"></i><h2>Research directions</h2></div>

<div class="pp-card">
  <span class="pp-year">1</span>
  <h3>Mathematical modelling and applied mathematics</h3>
  <p>My foundation is applied mathematics and mathematical modelling. My earlier research was about the calculus of variations, which is the study of functions that minimise an energy functional, and about numerical methods for approximating them. This kind of work is basic for understanding physical systems and structures when they are written as variational problems.</p>
  <p>In particular, I studied the Skyrme energy functional, looking at topological classes of maps called generalized twists and how they relate to the Euler–Lagrange equations. To approximate the solutions efficiently, I used advanced numerical techniques such as Fourier–Legendre spectral methods.</p>
  <div class="pp-tags"><span class="pp-tag">Calculus of variations</span><span class="pp-tag">Skyrme energy</span><span class="pp-tag">Spectral methods</span></div>
</div>

<div class="pp-card">
  <span class="pp-year">2</span>
  <h3>Computational modelling and numerical analysis</h3>
  <p>I also work on computational modelling with zero-dimensional lumped-parameter models. For example, I have used vessel models coupled with Windkessel terminal models to follow how blood flow, pressure and volume change over time. These models are systems of ordinary differential equations, which I solve with numerical methods such as forward Euler.</p>
  <p>To make the simulations accurate and reliable, I calibrate the models on synthetic data and estimate the key parameters with optimization. This work taught me how to turn a theoretical concept into a computational framework and to get predictive insight into real systems.</p>
  <div class="pp-tags"><span class="pp-tag">Blood flow</span><span class="pp-tag">Windkessel models</span><span class="pp-tag">ODEs</span><span class="pp-tag">Parameter estimation</span></div>
</div>

<div class="pp-card">
  <span class="pp-year">3</span>
  <h3>Machine learning and data-driven approaches</h3>
  <p>Building on this mathematical and computational background, I am now working on the link between machine learning and mathematics, to analyse complex datasets. This interdisciplinary direction strengthens my analytical skills and prepares me for current challenges in data science.</p>
  <p>I am especially interested in neural networks, and in particular physics-informed neural networks, applied to problems in biology, healthcare, environmental science and engineering. My aim is to build models that are understandable, robust and able to learn efficiently from structured or limited data, in settings where trust and reproducibility really matter.</p>
