Mozhgan Taghavi

Researcher in applied mathematics

and artificial intelligence

mojgan.taghavi88@gmail.com

I am an applied mathematician who enjoys learning, solving problems and doing research. My main interest is to connect mathematical theory with modern computational methods and artificial intelligence. Over the years, I have become more and more drawn to machine learning and data-driven approaches, because they allow us to turn mathematical ideas into practical answers for real-world problems.

Right now, I work on bringing mathematics and optimization together with AI-based techniques, in order to understand complex systems better. I like to approach a problem from both the analytical side and the computational side, and what motivates me most is the challenge of turning an abstract concept into a method that gives insight, prediction or real impact.

Mathematics

Calculus of variations, differential equations and numerical analysis, including spectral methods.

Machine learning

Neural networks, physics-informed neural networks and optimization for scientific problems.

Teaching

University lecturer and teaching assistant, with a passion for explaining complex ideas clearly.

TumorPINN Lab

My newest project brings together spectral solvers, CNNs and physics-informed neural networks to estimate the parameters of a tumour-growth model from noisy data, and then applies the same idea to real mouse-tumour measurements.
